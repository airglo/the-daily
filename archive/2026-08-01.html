📰 Big Story

The EU's AI transparency rules go live tomorrow

Starting Aug 2, Article 50 of the EU AI Act forces companies to clearly label AI-generated content — the first hard deadline of the world's most sweeping AI law.

Go deeperLess
  • What changes: Chatbots must disclose they're bots, and synthetic images, audio and video must be machine-readable as AI-made — deepfakes included.
  • The guardrails: The European Commission published draft Transparency Guidelines on May 8 spelling out how the marking-and-labeling has to work in practice.
  • Why it matters to you: Every US tech name in today's Big Tech list operates in the EU, so this quietly reshapes how Apple, Google and Meta ship AI features worldwide.
  • Bigger backdrop: The UN is running a parallel push for global AI governance, with officials warning of "catastrophic harm" absent coordinated rules.

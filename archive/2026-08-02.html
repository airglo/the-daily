📰 Big Story

California's AI-transparency law goes live today

As of August 2, big generative-AI providers must stamp provenance into their images, video and audio — a first real test of "prove it's AI" rules at scale.

Go deeperLess
  • Who it hits: SB 942 applies to providers with 1M+ California monthly users — which is effectively every frontier lab and consumer AI app.
  • What's required: embedded provenance metadata in AI output, a free public detection tool, and an option for users to add a visible AI label.
  • Why it matters: deepfakes and synthetic media have outrun disclosure norms; California is again setting the de facto national standard the way it did on emissions and privacy.
  • The catch: metadata can be stripped by a screenshot or re-encode, so enforcement teeth — not just the mandate — will decide if this actually changes anything.
